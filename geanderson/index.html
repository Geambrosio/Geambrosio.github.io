Biography

Geanderson Ambrosio is a researcher at CREAF in Barcelona, where he leads the automation of the BioAgora scenario database with large language models, and is a contributing author to the Future Pathways chapter of the second IPBES global assessment. He holds a PhD in applied economics and builds data tools and pipelines on land use and biodiversity, including the Mean Species Abundance (MSA) tool and the PICASSO SDG Dashboard. He has experience with global integrated assessment models, with a focus on their land-use components, such as MAgPIE and IMAGE-Land. Before CREAF he worked at Utrecht University, and was a visiting scholar at PBL Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency and a guest researcher at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research.

Published in Nature, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Reviews of Geophysics, Environmental Research Letters and Geoscientific Model Development.
